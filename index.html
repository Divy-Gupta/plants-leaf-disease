🌿 Plant Leaf Disease Detection

An AI-powered platform to detect plant leaf diseases instantly using image analysis and intelligent models.

📌 About the Project

Plant diseases are a major cause of crop loss worldwide. This application leverages Artificial Intelligence to analyze plant leaf images and identify possible diseases with useful insights.

✨ Key Features

  • 🌱 AI-powered plant leaf disease detection
  • 📷 Upload leaf images directly
  • ⚡ Fast and accurate results
  • 💡 Disease explanation and recommendations
  • 🌐 Works directly in the browser

🔍 How It Works

  1. User uploads a plant leaf image
  2. The image is processed by AI models
  3. Disease patterns are detected
  4. Results are displayed instantly

🛠️ Technology Stack

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: FastAPI
  • AI Integration: Groq LLM
  • Deployment: Streamlit Cloud
